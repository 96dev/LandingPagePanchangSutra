PanchangSutra

A daily Hindu Panchang and festival calendar for Android, in seven languages — calculated entirely on your phone.

  • Works offlineEvery tithi, nakshatra and sunrise is computed on the device. No internet needed.
  • No account, no trackingNothing to sign in to. The app collects no personal information.
  • SmallAbout 2 MB, so it opens instantly even on an older phone.
  • Your languageEach edition is written in its own language, not translated labels.

What the app shows

  • The day's Panchang — tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, paksha and the lunar month
  • Sunrise, sunset, moonrise and moonset
  • Choghadiya, Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika, Abhijit and Brahma Muhurta
  • A month calendar with festivals and vrats marked on the day
  • Festivals, vrats and public holidays for the year, by state
  • Sankashti Chaturthi, Ekadashi, Purnima and Amavasya lists with their times
  • Your birth tithi, and the date it falls on each year
  • Reminders you set yourself, and optional notifications for vrat days
  • A home-screen widget with today's tithi and timings

Panchang for your region. Each edition follows the calendar its readers use — Amanta or Purnimanta months, Vikram Samvat or Shalivahana Shaka years, and the Tamil solar months with their sixty-year cycle.
