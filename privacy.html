Privacy Policy

For the PanchangSutra apps. Last updated: 17 November 2026.

This policy explains what the PanchangSutra apps do with your information. It covers all editions of the app: Hindi, English (Almanac), Gujarati, Marathi, Tamil, Kannada and Telugu.

In short: the app does not ask you to sign in, does not collect your personal information, and calculates everything on your phone. The only outside service it uses is Google AdMob, which shows the advertisement at the bottom of the screen.

Information the app collects

None. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. It does not collect, store or send your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or any other personal information.

Information stored on your phone

These stay on your device, are never sent anywhere, and are deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Reminders you create: their title, note and time.
  • Your settings: chosen language, notification switches and notification time.
  • A chosen city or location for the Sankashti Chaturthi moonrise time, if you set one.

Location

The app asks for location permission only if you tap Use current location on the Sankashti Chaturthi screen. It is used on your phone to work out the moonrise time for your place. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can refuse the permission and choose a city from the built-in list instead; everything else in the app works the same.

Notifications and alarms

All notifications are switched off when you install the app. If you turn one on, the app sets an alarm on your phone and works out what to tell you locally. Nothing is sent over the internet to do this. The app uses the exact-alarm permission so that reminders arrive at the time you set.

Advertising

The app shows a banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. To do this, the AdMob software in the app sends information to Google, which may include:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • your IP address, and the approximate region it indicates
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • information about the advertisements shown and whether they were tapped

This is handled by Google under its own policy, not by us. Please read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can limit advertisement tracking on your phone under Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete or opt out of your advertising ID.

Internet use

The app uses the internet only to load advertisements. All Panchang calculations — tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise, sunset, moonrise, festivals, vrats and holidays — are done on your phone and work with no internet connection at all.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.
